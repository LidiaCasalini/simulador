A note on the model. The scenarios are simplified teaching situations. Pragmatic interpretation depends on context, tone, culture and the speakers’ shared history, so several answers here could be defended in other circumstances. The feedback is a starting point for discussion, not a universal rule of politeness. Classification follows Searle’s (1976) taxonomy, and the social variables come from Brown and Levinson (1987).
